DESIGN

The result is a packed bubble chart, titled Class of FY25, clustering NBFCs whose high-APR personal loan revenues grew sharply over the past year:

Bubble size maps to how many times a company's revenue multiplied between FY24 and FY25

Bubble colour distinguishes profitable companies (a green gradient, scaling with growth multiple) from loss-making ones (flat orange/red)

Hover state — surfaces company-level detail

The revenue in FY24 and FY25, growth multiple, year of establishment, and the loan brands operating under each NBFC.

A minimum size cap was applied to the bubbles. Without it, companies with 1–2x growth would be visually negligible next to outliers growing 6,000x+. The cap was a deliberate accessibility trade-off — sacrificing strict proportionality so that every company in the dataset remains visible and comparable.

The final visualisation was embedded into the story via an iframe, and published on 16th April.
