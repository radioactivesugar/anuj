A royal family's billion-dollar bet on Indian startups — without a winner.

09/04/2026 Duration: 1 Week Writer: Mrunmayee Kulkarni Information Design, Web Development eCharts, iFrame, CSS, JavaScript, Figma, GitHub, Google Gemini Live link: Intermission/Lightrock

"VC firm Lightrock used the Liechtenstein dynasty's fortune to prop up Waycool, Pharmeasy, and Dunzo. Then it slid into irrelevance."

The story tracked the scale of investment by Lightrock, a single investment firm, into a portfolio of companies over several years — examining how aggressively it deployed capital during the zero-interest-rate-policy (ZIRP) era, and how sharply that activity dropped off afterward.

The valuations and growth trajectories of the companies in its portfolio sat at the centre of the narrative.

DESIGN

Rather than a bar graph or a simple bubble chart, I built a clustered bubble chart. This gave the visualisation a sense of dimension per company.

Bubble size mapped to the company's valuation scale (under $100M, $100M–$500M, $500M+)

In-bubble label Lightrock's actual investment amount, where disclosed

Hover state — surfaces company-level detail

Surfaces company name, latest valuation, total funding raised, and Lightrock's investment amount.

The final visualisation was embedded into the story via an iframe, and published on 16th April.
