    Aashirvaad defines packaged atta

    Aashirvaad Rest of market
    2015
    The organised wheat-flour segment was worth only about Rs 3,500 crore — a sliver of India's Rs 30,000 crore atta market. With few branded rivals, Aashirvaad held roughly 75% and owned most of it.
    2018
    As packaged atta caught on, the branded market expanded fast and fragmented. New entrants took slices, so Aashirvaad's share fell to about 28% even as its sales climbed past Rs 4,200 crore.
    2026
    In a now-mature, much larger branded market, Aashirvaad pulled back to clear leadership at over 45%.
