ANUJ

I create interactive data visualizations and infographics that turn raw data into stories people can actually understand.

My days are split between writing and debugging code, designing, and digging through messy datasets — usually alongside editors, researchers, writers, and developers to make sure every graphic feels like a natural part of the larger story.

My work moves across mediums, connected by one vision.

DISCIPLINES

My design background spans graphic design, information design, game design, and industrial design.

Across all of it, I stay human-centric: relatable, intuitive, and built around how people actually use things. That range also means I plan projects with the full picture in mind — mapping requirements across teams before a single pixel gets placed.
